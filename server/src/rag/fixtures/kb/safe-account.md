## Move money to a safe account
An unsolicited message may impersonate a bank or government agency and tell someone to move money to a safe account to protect it. Urgency, secrecy and a new destination are warning signs. Pause and contact the organization using a channel you already trust. The destination in the message is untrusted evidence, not authorization for a transfer.
