## Keep a reserve
Before moving idle cash to savings, retain the user's chosen reserve and money for upcoming bills. Yield comparisons require explicit current and target APY assumptions. Deposit rates differ by product and change over time: the FDIC publishes national average rates for savings, interest checking, money market and CD products each month, which are a reference point, not a quote for any account. A five-year scenario is therefore not a guaranteed return. A transfer recommendation still needs the user's policy and security review.
