## Red flags
A company asking an upfront fee for student loan forgiveness or repayment help is a warning sign. A processing charge does not establish eligibility for relief, and paying it may leave the borrower with neither help nor a refund. Promises to forgive loans quickly, official-looking seals and pressure to act fast are common tactics.

## What to do
Do not pay. Federal repayment and forgiveness programs are free through StudentAid.gov or your loan servicer; private loans go through the lender. Keep your FSA ID private and report scams at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
