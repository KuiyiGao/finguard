## Red flags
An unexpected caller claims a relative is in trouble and presses for immediate, secret payment. Caller identity and a familiar voice alone are not sufficient evidence.

## What to do
Pause the payment. Contact the relative or another family member using a previously known number; do not use the number supplied by the caller.
