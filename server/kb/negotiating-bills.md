## Compare before negotiating
Prepare a recurring-bill review using the current bill and a comparable offer. For internet service, the broadband label helps check the monthly price, speed, data allowance and promotion terms. Include equipment charges, taxes and any cost of leaving the existing contract in the comparison.

## Ask the provider
Use those details to ask whether a lower-priced plan or recurring discount is available. Request written confirmation of its duration, later price and service changes. A successful discussion may reduce a bill, but savings remain conditional on the accepted terms. FinGuard does not negotiate with real providers in this demo.
