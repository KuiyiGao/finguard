## Red flags
Watch the payment instructions as well as the story. A stranger claiming to represent support, a business or an agency may insist on gift card codes, cryptocurrency or a wire before resolving an alleged problem. A demand for a specific payment method after unexpected contact is a warning sign, even when the sender uses a familiar name.

## What to do
Refuse the requested payment and keep card codes private. Check the story through an independently found contact. If you paid, promptly notify the gift card issuer, bank or payment service and ask what recovery options exist. Preserve the transaction details and report at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. A report or recovery request does not guarantee money back.
