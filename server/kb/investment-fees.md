## What an expense ratio is
Read a fund's prospectus fee table when reviewing its expense ratio. The ratio expresses annual operating costs relative to the fund's average net assets. Those costs reduce fund assets, so a statement may not show an individual charge for them.

## Why small differences add up
When otherwise comparable investments earn the same return before costs, different expense ratios leave different amounts invested. Repeated costs can also reduce later growth. Compare costs alongside the fund's strategy and risks; a cheaper fund does not guarantee a better outcome. Hypothetical fee-drag charts describe assumptions rather than future performance.
