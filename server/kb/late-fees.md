## Upcoming payment
Set a reminder with enough time for the chosen payment method to reach the provider. For a credit card, read the statement's due date and time zone and check the issuer's cutoff for that method; posting a letter on the due date may be too late. Other bills have their own terms. If a card fee appears, ask the issuer to review it without assuming a waiver.

A past late payment fee is evidence of a historical cost, not the current provider's fee schedule. Verify terms before stating an avoided fee. Do not add a one-off possible fee to recurring monthly savings.
