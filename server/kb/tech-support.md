## Red flags
An unsolicited support contact claims a device is compromised and asks for remote access or urgent payment. A warning on screen is not proof of a legitimate support relationship.

## What to do
Close the contact and seek support using the vendor official channel. Do not install remote-access software at an unexpected caller request.
