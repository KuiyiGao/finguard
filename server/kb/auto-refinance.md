## When refinancing helps
A car loan with a high interest rate may be refinanced into a loan at a lower rate. Refinancing means retiring the original loan in full.

## Check the costs first
The loan contract and state law decide whether an early payoff brings a prepayment penalty; some states ban that penalty on certain loans. Ask about any penalty when signing, and count it, plus any new loan fee, when comparing the payment and total cost of each loan.
