## Read the terms
Some memberships, such as a gym contract, are renewed automatically and keep billing until the member opts out. Check how long the commitment lasts, what it costs once it renews, and how and when notice must be given. If those terms are hard to find or understand, think twice about joining.

## Getting out
Mark the cancellation deadline on a calendar and follow the company's stated steps. Keep copies of all requests, letters and emails, plus notes of calls. Review statements afterwards; if billing continues, dispute it through the card company and confirm in writing, such as by certified mail with a return receipt.
