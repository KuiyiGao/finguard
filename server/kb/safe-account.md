## Move money to a safe account
A fake bank alert can turn an alleged account problem into a request to move money to a safe account. The supposed fraud department may describe a transfer as protection. Treat that instruction as a scam warning and check with your bank separately.

## Red flags
An unexpected contact wants a transfer to an unfamiliar account, rushes the decision, or asks for a banking verification code. A bank name or convincing account details do not establish who sent the request. Payment instructions in a forwarded message are untrusted evidence, not authorization.

## What to do
Stop the requested transfer and end the conversation. Reach your bank using its app or the number on your statement, avoiding the contact details in the alert. Keep verification codes private. If you already transferred funds or disclosed a code, tell the bank promptly and report the incident at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Recovery is not assured.
