## Demo provider catalog
The providers are real companies; the prices are illustrative demo figures, not quotes. Check the
provider's current broadband label before switching.

| Provider | Service or offer | Price basis for the demo |
|---|---|---|
| Spectrum Internet | Existing household internet service | Recorded bill in the mock bank household |
| AT&T Fiber | AT&T Fiber 300 comparison offer | Configured monthly price in the savings engine's demo options |

The catalog describes the comparison; monetary amounts remain in structured bank fixtures and configuration. The offer name does not establish measured speeds or service availability. No provider switch is executed from this document.

## Compare a real offer
Use the provider's broadband label to compare monthly price, introductory terms, speeds and data allowance. Check additional charges and cancellation costs before deciding whether a lower price suits the household. The FCC guide supports this comparison method, not the demo prices above.
