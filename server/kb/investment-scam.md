## Red flags
No one can promise guaranteed profits or big returns. Anyone who guarantees daily or monthly returns, or calls a crypto platform risk free, is showing a warning sign, even with testimonials or famous names attached. Demands to be paid in cryptocurrency are another.

## What to do
Do not invest through a platform or wallet found via an unexpected message. Unlike card payments, crypto payments usually cannot be reversed or disputed. Check the seller and the offer independently first.
